# bachelor-thesis
Movement Stimulation through Social Media: The Tweeted Perspective and Road Safety Movement in Bangladesh


Abstract—

It is a very common matter that due to the huge
popularity of social media, any matter around us, especially any
movement, is greatly stimulated by the impact of social media.
We can see a parallel contribution on Twitter, Facebook or some
other popular social media on every protest. We have noticed the
power of hashtags in all recent movements. In this paper we will
try to show the impact of social media on protest by including the
example data of Bangladesh road safety movement. For Ground
protest information we collect national and international
newspapers front page coverage as references. Another
information source was Twitter. We will use a social media
monitoring tool to collect Twitter hashtags information. After
that we will made a comparative analysis of the newspaper’s
coverage as ground protest nature, and Twitter hashtags as social
media impact. Finally, we will try to show that how the social
media, Twitter influence the ground movement.


Keywords-Twitter data analysis; road safety movement;
Bangladesh; hashtags; stimulate movement; social media impact





In this Research we studied how Road safety movement
spread through Twitter like the ground protest across all over
the country. After a comparative study of these two results,
newspaper and social media, we reached a point that the
ground protest and protest on Twitter go on parallels most of
the time. We also can conclude that social media influences on
stimulating any movement. It indicates that if twitter
contributes to the protest then success can come too fast and it
keeps the attention of all citizens of the country and world as
well. Because there is no geographical boundary in Social
media.
